## Key facts
- Born in Pistoia (Italy), 15 August 1962
- PhD in Physics, Sapienza Rome, 1988–1991
- Researcher 1990 · Associate Professor 2000 · Full Professor 2018 (Condensed Matter Physics)
- 250+ publications, 10,000+ citations (Dec 2025); h-index 49 (WoK) / 51 (Google Scholar)
  - (Unicam text, May 2022: 200+ pubs, 8000+ citations, h-index 43 / 46)
- Chairman XAFS14 and LAM14; co-editor of 3 proceedings books, 1 EPJ Special Topics, 1 GnXAS book
- Invited scientist: SSRL, ESRF, Japan (Aichi, Toyama, Kumamoto)
- Invited professor: Paris-Sud (XI), UPMC (Paris VI, international chair), Rennes
- Past president of SILS (Italian Synchrotron Radiation Society); past chair of IXAS
  - (Unicam text, 2022: "current president" of SILS and ex-officio chair of IXAS)
- IXAS Outstanding Achievement Award (Stern Prize), August 2015
- SILS career award, September 2025
- Coordinator of the Camerino XAS group; co-author of GnXAS
- TIMEX project at FERMI@Elettra; consultant ELETTRA/FERMI 2007–2013
- Italian unit leader in EU project SIRBATT (Li-ion batteries); PRIN projects
- PhD programme coordinator (Physics, Earth and Materials Sciences) 2017–2023
- Head of the Physics Division 2015–2025
- Current: coordinator of Bachelor/Master studies in Physics; Rector's delegate for International Research Networks
